# ClaimLens Fictional Motor Insurance Policy (Standard Comprehensive Cover)

**Policy Form Reference:** CL-MOT-2026-COMP  
**Coverage Scope:** Private Cars & Two-Wheelers (Accident, Theft & Comprehensive Own-Damage)

---

## Section I: Policy Coverage & Scope

### Clause C-01: Accidental Loss and Damage Coverage
The insurer will indemnify the policyholder against sudden, accidental loss or damage to the insured vehicle caused by collision, overturning, or external violent impact during the policy period within the geographical boundaries specified in the policy schedule.

### Clause C-02: Theft and Total Burglary Coverage
In the event of total theft or burglary of the insured vehicle, the insurer will pay the Insured Declared Value (IDV) minus applicable deductible, provided prompt notification (within 48 hours) and certified police First Information Report (FIR) are submitted. Ignition keys must not have been left inside an unattended vehicle.

### Clause C-03: Fire, Explosion, and Lightning Damage
Direct loss or damage caused by fire, spontaneous combustion, self-ignition, lightning, or explosion is covered under own-damage coverage, provided fire does not result from unapproved electrical modifications.

### Clause C-04: Natural Calamities and Disasters
Damage resulting from flood, typhoon, hurricane, storm, tempest, inundation, cyclone, earthquake, or landslide is indemnified subject to policy limits. Engine hydrostatic lock caused by restarting engine in deep water is excluded unless hydro-lock add-on cover is active.

---

## Section II: Exclusions & Repudiation Conditions

### Clause C-05: Third-Party Liability Exclusions
Own-damage claims do not cover liability incurred to third-party property or persons unless specifically processed under Section III (Third-Party Legal Liability).

### Clause C-06: Intoxication and Drunk Driving Exclusion
The insurer is not liable for any loss or damage incurred while the driver operating the vehicle is under the influence of intoxicating liquor, alcohol, or narcotic drugs.

### Clause C-07: Valid Driving Licence Requirement
Any loss or damage sustained while the vehicle is driven by any person who does not hold a valid, effective driving licence or learner's permit appropriate for the vehicle class is strictly excluded.

---

## Section III: Claims Procedure & Required Evidence

### Clause C-08: Claim Reporting Window
The policyholder must notify the insurer within 7 calendar days for accidental damage claims, and within 48 hours for vehicle theft claims, unless justifiable reasons prevent timely notice.

### Clause C-09: Mandatory Claim Documentation
Every claim submission must contain a minimum set of 3 mandatory evidence items:
1. Completed Claim Form
2. Detailed Repair Estimate or Police FIR
3. Customer Incident Description

### Clause C-10: Police FIR Requirement for Theft and Malicious Damage
A certified copy of the Police First Information Report (FIR) is compulsory for all claims involving theft, burglary, malicious damage, or severe third-party involvement.

### Clause C-11: Itemized Repair Estimate Requirements
For accidental damage, an itemized repair estimate from an authorized garage detailing parts replacement costs, labor charges, and GST invoice is mandatory prior to repair authorization.

---

## Section IV: IDV, Deductible & Loss Settlement

### Clause C-12: Insured Declared Value (IDV) Limit
The maximum liability of the insurer for any own-damage claim or total loss is capped strictly at the Insured Declared Value (IDV) specified in the policy schedule.

### Clause C-13: Standard Compulsory Deductible
Every own-damage claim settlement is subject to a compulsory excess deductible of ₹1,000 for two-wheelers and ₹2,000 for private cars, deductible from the final payout.

### Clause C-14: Constructive Total Loss Condition
A vehicle will be considered a Constructive Total Loss (CTL) if the aggregate cost of retrieval and repair exceeds 75% of the Insured Declared Value (IDV).

### Clause C-15: Partial Damage Depreciation Rates
For partial damage repairs, standard depreciation rates shall apply: Rubber/Nylon/Plastic parts (50%), Glass parts (0%), Fibreglass (30%), Metal parts (0% to 50% depending on vehicle age).

---

## Section V: Vehicle Use & Identity Standards

### Clause C-16: Excluded Performance Modifications and CNG/LPG Conversions
Damage to unauthorized speed modifications, unapproved CNG/LPG kits, or non-factory accessories not specified in the policy schedule is excluded from coverage.

### Clause C-17: Unauthorized Commercial or Illegal Vehicle Use
Coverage is void if the private vehicle was used for commercial hire, racing, speed testing, illegal haulage, or any criminal activity at the time of loss.

### Clause C-18: Lapse and Policy Expiry
No claim shall be payable if the date of incident occurs outside the active policy inception and expiry timestamps specified in the policy schedule.

### Clause C-19: Vehicle Identity and Chassis Verification
The registration number, chassis number, and engine number on all submitted documents (Claim Form, FIR, Repair Bill) must match the vehicle records exactly.

---

## Section VI: Audit, Escalation & Dispute Resolution

### Clause C-20: Misrepresentation and Misleading Information
If any submitted evidence or statement contains material misrepresentations or false dates, the claim will be escalated to a human investigator without alleging fraud.

### Clause C-21: Salvage Property Rights
Upon payment of CTL or total loss claim, the insurer retains legal ownership rights over the damaged vehicle salvage.

### Clause C-22: Mandatory Spot Inspection and Investigation
The insurer reserves the right to order an independent spot inspection or surveyor audit for claims exceeding ₹50,000 or involving physical injury.

### Clause C-23: Mandatory Human Investigator Escalation
Any claim exhibiting cross-document contradictions (such as conflicting incident dates, registration numbers, or damage locations) MUST be escalated to a human investigator.

### Clause C-24: Handling of Incomplete Information
Claims lacking essential evidence (such as missing repair bills or incomplete claim forms) must be placed on 'REQUEST INFORMATION' status.
