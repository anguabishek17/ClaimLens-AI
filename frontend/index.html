Everything you need to process claims faster

A complete AI pipeline — from raw document upload to final recommendation.

Multi-Document Upload

Upload Claim Forms, Incident Descriptions, FIRs, and Repair Estimates. Supports TXT, PDF, and image files in a single drag-and-drop interface.

Deterministic Policy Checks

Every claim is checked against a structured policy rule engine — covering timely reporting, document completeness, and coverage eligibility.

AI Contradiction Detection

Gemini AI cross-references dates, amounts, vehicle details, and damage descriptions across all uploaded documents to surface factual discrepancies.

Escalation Intelligence

When contradictions or high-risk signals are detected, ClaimLens automatically flags the claim for human review with a priority level and suggested action.

Policy Clause Retrieval

Semantic search over the full policy knowledge base surfaces the exact clauses relevant to each claim, with reasoning grounded in policy text.

Structured Audit Trail

Every recommendation includes a complete breakdown — rule results, evidence findings, applicable clauses, and a human-readable summary for compliance.
